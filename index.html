Scientific Calculator

Built for technical calculations, business estimates, and fast memory work with a sticky, easy-to-scan layout. Keyboard-friendly: type numbers, operators, press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete, and Escape to clear.

How to use

Root

Tap √, then enter a value and press =.

Sine

Tap sin, enter the angle, and press =.

Tangent

Tap tan, add the angle, and press =.

Power

Use xʸ for exponent work like 2 xʸ 3.

Cosine

Tap cos, enter the angle, and press =.

Factorial

Enter a number, tap n!, then = (e.g., 5 n! = 120).

Parentheses

Use ( and ) to group expressions, e.g., (2+3)×4.

Reciprocal

Enter a value, tap 1/x to get its reciprocal.

Absolute value

Tap |x| to get the absolute value of a number.

Modulo

Use mod for remainder, e.g., 10 mod 3 = 1.
